# Tourism Datasets & Schemas

## Dataset Catalogue
- `data/states.json`: List of 28 Indian States and 8 Union Territories with capitals, regions, and representative coordinates.
- `data/cities.json`: Major metropolitan hubs, tier-2 tourism centers, and connectivity hubs.
- `data/india_tourism.json`: Curated destinations across India with historical summaries, entry fees, and categories.
- `data/railway_stations.json`: Suburban railway and inter-city terminals with railway lines and GPS coordinates.
- `data/heritage/monuments.json`: High-fidelity historical metadata for 3D monument visualizations.
- Regional datasets: `data/mumbai/places.json`, `data/delhi/places.json`, `data/rajasthan/places.json`, `data/kerala/places.json`, `data/maharashtra/places.json`.

## Verified Data Sources
1. Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) official archives.
2. Incredible India - Ministry of Tourism, Government of India.
3. Indian Railways (IRCTC) transit hubs and fare schedules.
4. Municipal Corporation transit and meter fare regulations.
